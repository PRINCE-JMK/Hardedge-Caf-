Our Story

A neighbourhood
gem in Penge

Hardedge Café has been a beloved fixture on 113 High Street for years. Run by passionate owner Mehdi and a warm team, we serve home-cooked food with big portions, fair prices and a relaxed atmosphere that keeps locals coming back day after day.

From the legendary Full Monty breakfast to homemade quiche, fresh omelettes and rich pasta — everything is cooked with care and served with a smile.
